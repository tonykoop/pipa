# Concept Image Prompt Brief

Use this only for non-authoritative concept imagery.

Required prompt constraints:

- Concept image only; not fabrication authority.
- Do not include cut-ready dimensions, hole locations, fret coordinates, bridge
  coordinates, scale lengths, CAD grids, or toolpaths.
- Fabrication dimensions are controlled by `DXF-001` and later measured CAD or
  reference templates, not by generated images.

## Prompt

Create a respectful concept image of a pipa-inspired prototype on a clean bench:
a pear-shaped front silhouette, short neck, four strings, provisional bridge,
visible placeholder fret bands, and warm natural wood finish. Show the design as
a prototype review object with subtle tags or sketch overlays that say
"outline review", "fret geometry unresolved", and "rear shell not modeled".
Avoid dimensional callouts, rulers, cut paths, CNC fixtures, or anything that
looks like final fabrication authority.

## Negative Constraints

- No measured dimensions.
- No CNC-ready plan view.
- No fret-coordinate table.
- No rear shell geometry claims.
- No pegbox side-profile claim.
