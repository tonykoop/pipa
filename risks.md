# Starter Risks

## Acoustic And Setup Risks

- A nominal 725 mm scale may not match the target reference pipa.
  Mitigation: measure a physical reference before CAD promotion.
- Equal-tempered fret math may not match actual fret heights, compensation, or
  player setup.
  Mitigation: record reference fret positions and run an intonation check.
- String tension cannot be trusted until gauges and unit weights are verified.
  Mitigation: source a real string set and run a tension calculation before
  loading a soundboard.

## Fabrication Risks

- Body bowl geometry may be too complex for the selected process.
  Mitigation: choose carved, laminated, molded, or assembled construction only
  after a process review.
- Soundboard thickness and bridge footprint may fail structurally or sound
  muted.
  Mitigation: build coupons for tap, deflection, and bridge-load checks.
- Fret material and attachment method may cause height drift or intonation
  errors.
  Mitigation: prototype a short fretboard section before full layout.

## Sourcing Risks

- Published dimensions and online listings may mix modern, regional, student,
  or performance-grade variants.
  Mitigation: keep all sourced values reference-only until tied to a measured
  target.
- Supplier price, lead time, and stock can change.
  Mitigation: verify at purchase time and avoid hard purchase claims in the
  packet.

## Scope Risks

- A visually plausible pipa packet could be mistaken for a build packet.
  Mitigation: keep L1, not-build-ready, and measurement-required language in
  README, design notes, and CSV rows until validation evidence exists.
