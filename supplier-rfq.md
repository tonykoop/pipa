# Supplier And Reviewer RFQ

Provide measurement or review support for a pipa-inspired L2 prototype packet.

CNC cutting.

- Fret count, fret heights, and placement basis.
- Pegbox side profile and string break angle.
- Rear shell depth or cross-section measurements.
