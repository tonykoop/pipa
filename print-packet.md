# Pipa Image-Grounded Prototype Print Packet

Status: L2 V5 build-packet candidate.

## Build Authority

`cad/v2-dxf-starter.dxf` and `drawings/pipa-v5-starter.dxf` are the only
fabrication-authority artifacts in this packet, and only for prototype
front-layout review. Generated imagery and prompt briefs are concept-only.
`cad/pipa.scad` and `wolfram/fret-scale-study.wl` are source-only scaffolds
until measured inputs and runtime evidence exist.

## What This Packet Supports

- flat silhouette mule;
- visual review of body/neck outline;
- measurement planning;
- MCP/provenance and validation-loop review;
- source-only fret/scale study setup;
- BOM and sourcing preparation;
- concept-image prompting with authority safeguards.

## What This Packet Blocks

- final fret cutting;
- rear shell carving;
- pegbox side-profile fabrication;
- CNC body machining;
- production quote as final geometry.

## Validation Summary

Use `validation.csv` as the gate list. The packet is ready for visual authority
validation and manifest checks, but measured reference-member review is still
required before final CAD.
