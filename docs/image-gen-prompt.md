# Image-Gen Prompts

Concept image only; not fabrication authority. Do not include cut-ready dimensions, hole locations, or toolpaths.

## Hero Prompt

A finished pipa-inspired lute prototype photographed as a real physical object: a teardrop / pear-shaped front silhouette tapering to a short fretted neck and a compact pegbox at the top, with four strings running from the pegbox down the centerline to a small provisional bridge block low on the soundboard. The body reads as a single warm-toned wood instrument with a smooth soundboard face, the neck carrying a row of low fret bands, and four tuning pegs implied at the head. Soft natural wood grain and a light satin finish suggest the material; realistic scale cues come from the four slender strings and the pegbox proportions of a held lute roughly arm-length tall. Clean neutral light-gray studio background, soft directional studio light, 3/4 product-photography angle, ~50mm lens, high detail, photorealistic, no text labels.

## In-Context Prompt

The pipa prototype staged on a clean luthier's workbench as a review object: the pear-shaped front silhouette resting face-up beside a full-scale paper outline print and simple layout tools, warm window light raking across the soundboard, a few wood shavings and pencils suggesting an active bench. Short neck with placeholder fret bands and the four-string centerline visible, the rear shell left in soft shadow since its depth is unresolved. Natural light, environmental storytelling, shallow depth of field, concept visualization only, no measurement callouts.

## Detail Prompt

Macro close-up of the signature mechanism: the short neck and head of the pipa where four strings converge over a row of low placeholder fret bands toward the pegbox, then sweep down to the small provisional bridge block on the soundboard. Show wood grain, the satin finish on the fret face, the fine strings catching light, and the subtle step where neck meets body. Raking light, high detail, shallow depth of field, no measurements, no dimension lines.

## Negative / Avoid

No text, logos, watermarks, or fake maker marks; no dimension callouts, ruler scales, fret-coordinate tables, CAD grids, or toolpaths; no carved rear-shell or pegbox side-profile claims presented as authoritative; no impossible mechanisms; anatomically plausible hands if a player is shown.
